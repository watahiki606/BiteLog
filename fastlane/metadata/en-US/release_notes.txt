You can now log salt equivalent and saturated fat.

- The food form has a new Other Nutrients section for salt equivalent and saturated fat. Leave them blank if the label doesn't show them
- When you photograph a nutrition label, the analysis reads these two as well
- Daily totals and statistics show them. When some foods have no value, BiteLog tells you how many foods the total covers
- Add your sex and birth date in Profile to compare them with the dietary goals in Statistics
- CSV export adds two columns at the end

When you register a food from a photo or by hand, BiteLog now tells you if a food with the same name and unit already exists.
