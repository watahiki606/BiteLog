BiteLog now covers many more nutrients, with clearer screens.

- Log salt equivalent, saturated fat, potassium, calcium, iron, vitamins A, D, and C, and more. Leave a value blank if the label doesn't show it
- See 20 more nutrients, such as phosphorus, zinc, iodine, selenium, and B vitamins
- Choose from 2,538 foods in Japan's national food composition tables when you search for a food. They appear after your own foods
- The daily total and Statistics show nutrients as cards with rings. Open Other Nutrients once to see them all
- Add your sex and birth date in Profile to see each nutrient as a share of the national reference value
- Tap a nutrient in Statistics to read what it does, what happens with too little or too much, and foods rich in it, with sources
- Photo analysis now splits a dish into ingredients and weights and calculates values from the composition tables
- The food and log entry editors now make it clear which fields you can edit
- Carbohydrates, sugar, and dietary fiber now have the same names on every screen
- CSV export adds the new nutrients as columns at the end

When you register a food from a photo or by hand, BiteLog now tells you if a food with the same name and unit already exists.
