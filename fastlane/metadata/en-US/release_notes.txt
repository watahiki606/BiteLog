BiteLog now covers many more nutrients.

- Log salt equivalent, saturated fat, potassium, calcium, magnesium, iron, and vitamins A, D, and C. Leave a value blank if the label doesn't show it
- Choose from 2,538 foods in the Standard Tables of Food Composition in Japan when you search for a food. They appear after your own foods
- Photo analysis now splits a dish into ingredients and weights, and takes the values from the table
- See 20 more nutrients, such as phosphorus, zinc, and B vitamins, in food details, the daily total, and Statistics
- Add your sex and birth date in Profile to compare nutrients with the dietary reference intakes in Statistics
- The food form now follows the label: carbohydrates, then dietary fiber
- CSV export adds the new nutrients as columns at the end

When you register a food from a photo or by hand, BiteLog now tells you if a food with the same name and unit already exists.
