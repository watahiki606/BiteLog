New in 2.6: body composition scale

BiteLog can receive weight and body composition from a TANITA RD-902 Bluetooth scale. The feature is optional. Every other part of the app works without the scale, and nothing related to the scale appears on the log screen until a scale has been registered.

Where to find it: Settings > Data Management > Scale Setup.

- Personal Data: height, sex, and birth date entered in BiteLog. The scale needs these to calculate body composition.
- Registration: pairs the scale with BiteLog over Bluetooth.
- After registration, stepping on the scale shows the measurement on that day's log screen.

Bluetooth is used only to talk to the scale. Body composition values are calculated by the scale itself; BiteLog only receives and displays them.

Without the scale, searching ends with a "scale not found" message. A video of the full flow on a real device is available on request.
